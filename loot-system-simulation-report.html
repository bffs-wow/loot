Grounded Reality: The 16-Slot Saturation Dynamic

In real raiding, players do not have infinite demand. A character only has 16 equipment slots. Once a raider wins their ~8 Major items (weapons, trinkets, tier tokens, chest, legs) and ~14 Minor pieces, they are fully saturated and step aside. This means everyone eventually gets geared—what truly separates loot systems is Acquisition Velocity (how quickly each archetype reaches full BiS) and whether hoarding unfairly accelerates major weapons ahead of balanced teammates.

Key Discoveries from Grounded Reality

1. The Hoarder's Timing Advantage (Standard EPGP)

Under standard EPGP (where minor items cost GP), Lurkin (Hoarder) finishes all 8 Major items at Week 3.9, while Goatlord (Balanced Veteran) finishes at Week 5.7. Hoarding buys nearly 2 weeks of weapon priority at the cost of minor armor upgrades.

2. Zero-GP Minor Items Equalize Velocity Perfectly

When Minor items cost 0 GP (Free), the hoarder rationally bids on minor items too! Lurkin and Goatlord reach their 4th Major item at Week 3.0 and their 8th Major item at Week 5.7—100% identical velocity. Hoarding as an exploit is completely eliminated.

3. Demand Falloff Powers Rapid Recruit Catch-up

Because veterans saturate their major slots by Week 5–6, Newrecruit (joining Week 5 with 2-week trial) wins their 1st Major at Week 7.0, 4th Major at Week 7.4, and finishes full BiS by Week 8.5! The natural drop in veteran demand accelerates recruit gearing effortlessly.

4. The Standby Bench Morale Shield

Nadzia (Priest, representing high bench count) finishes their 8 Major items at the exact same pace as active raiders (Week 5.7), proving that 100% standby credit online at raid start completely protects benched players from falling behind in gear.
