Key Discoveries Across the Knobs

1. The "Zero-GP Minor" Anti-Hoarder Fix

Under standard EPGP, hoarders win +75% more major weapons and trinkets (34.7 vs 20.5) by passing on minor upgrades. When Minor Items cost 0 GP (Free), the hoarder advantage drops to 0% (22.0 vs 22.1). Balanced veterans get all armor pieces without sacrificing weapon priority!

2. The 6-Week Rolling Horizon (Hard Falloff)

Replacing exponential percentage decay with a 6-week rolling horizon produces virtually identical mathematical distribution, but removes recurring Tuesday database maintenance and aligns with the guild's 6-week attendance window.

3. Standby Equity & Dedicated Bench

Standby raiders like Nadzia (Priest, 6 bench credits) who are confirmed online at raid start achieve identical gear progression to 100% active raiders, completely eliminating the morale penalty of sitting out for composition.

4. Additive Boost Punishes Flakiness & Recruits

In Additive Boost (+120), a 70% casual like Seph wins only 31% of veteran loot, and new recruits like Newrecruit wait until Week 8.4 to win their first item. In EPGP, casuals get 70% loot and recruits win items right after trial.
